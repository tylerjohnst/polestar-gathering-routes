Polestar Community: Grinding (PolestarPack_Grinding)
Author: SDG

Includes Mining Routes, Herbalism Routes, and Community Grinding Spots.
In Polestar's guide picker, look for Leveling / Community Grinding or search
for "grinding". Each of the 11 locations is a separately selectable guide,
available to either faction, with its recommended level range in the title.

Grinding guides show the supplied mob types, drops / nearby resources,
add / social pull risk, and solo difficulty. Follow the location waypoint
and earn XP until the listed upper level. Already at that level? The step
does not apply. These guides are independent choices, not a travel circuit.

Zones already covered by Polestar's built-in XP-grinding goals are omitted:
The Barrens, Hillsbrad Foothills, Thousand Needles, Dustwallow Marsh,
The Riverglades, Tanaris, The Hinterlands, Feralas, and Un'Goro Crater.
This is zone-level deduplication, not an exact coordinate or mob comparison.
Multiple distinct community spots within an otherwise uncovered zone remain.

Coordinates are WoW Forever live zone-map percentages, not Classic Era
coordinates. Locations, drops, and difficulty are community reports and
have not been verified in game. Ore/herbs require gathering, and leather
requires Skinning. The guides do not guarantee safety or resource spawns.

Edit GrindGuides.lua to maintain the grinding locations. Broad mob groups
use a location waypoint and a native level goal, without fabricated NPC
IDs or spawn areas; they do not provide mob-specific targeting/navigation.
Routes.lua is generated and is unrelated to the grinding definitions.
